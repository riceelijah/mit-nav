# Core user journey (P3 beta)

A Course 6 student in Simmons Hall has a lecture in 32-123 in the Stata Center and a sprained ankle. They open MIT Navigator and sign in with their MIT class account through Commons (a visitor without one creates a username and password instead). Their saved preference for step-free routes is already on, and so is "Use my location". They type "32-1" and pick 32-123 Lecture Hall from the search list. The app lists the steps, about 1.2 km in all, and draws the route on a map: out of Simmons, a few outdoor walks toward Building 35, then indoors through 37, 39, 38, 34 and 36 into Stata. They star 32-123 as a favorite and save the trip as "To lecture".

As they walk, the steps tick off on their own as they reach each building, and the next step is highlighted. Inside, where a phone can't tell where it is, they tap "Done" on each indoor step. On the way they take a wrong turn toward the Media Lab. After two location readings far from the line, the app says they went off course and gives a new route from the nearest building.

At Stata the elevator they need is out of order. They press "Flag blocked" on that step and type "doors won't open". The report appears under open problems with their username, and step-free directions for everyone now avoid that elevator. When they reach the hall, they press "I've arrived", and their travels show one more trip and the estimated distance. Later a classmate signs in and marks the elevator clear. The whole elevator comes back, and the report is resolved.
