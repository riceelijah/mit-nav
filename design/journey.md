# Core user journey (P3 beta)

A Course 6 student in Simmons Hall has a lecture in 32-123 in the Stata Center and a sprained ankle. On their phone they open MIT Navigator and sign in with their MIT class account through Commons (a visitor without one creates a username and password). Their saved preferences already turn on step-free routes, location, and distances in walking minutes.

They type "32-1" and pick 32-123 Lecture Hall. The destination appears as a door plaque, with a 7-step, about 14-minute route that follows the sidewalks of Vassar Street, goes in through Fairchild (Building 36), and takes the corridors into Stata. The map draws it along the real paths. They star 32-123 and save the trip as "To lecture". Before leaving, they tap Restroom; the nearest is on Simmons' first floor, about a minute away.

As they walk, the steps outside tick off as they reach each building, walked parts of the line turn grey, and the step they're on is highlighted on the map and in the list. Indoors they tap Done on each step, and the list scrolls to the next. Zooming in on Stata shows its first-floor plan, with the lecture hall highlighted under the route and a floor switcher on the map. On the way they take a wrong turn toward the Media Lab; after two location readings far from the line, the app reroutes them from the nearest building.

In Stata the elevator they would need later is out of order, so they press "Report a problem" on that step and type "doors won't open". The report shows under Problems with their username, and step-free directions for everyone avoid that elevator. At the hall they press "I've arrived", and Saved shows one more trip and the walking it took. Later a classmate signs in and marks the elevator clear; the whole elevator comes back and the report is resolved.
