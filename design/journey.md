# Core user journey (P2)

A visitor arriving at Simmons Hall opens the app on an empty database that has been loaded with the building map made from the floor plans. They choose the Main Lobby Entrance as their starting point and the 8th floor of C tower (8C) as their destination, and tick "Step-free route only" because they cannot manage stairs. The app shows three numbered steps that use the C elevator. The visitor reaches the elevator, finds it out of service, and presses "Flag blocked" on the step that says to get into it, adding their name and a reason. The directions immediately change to use a different elevator instead, and the problem appears in the list of open reports. Later someone sees that the elevator works again and marks it clear, which resolves the report and restores the original route.
