# Core user journey (P3 beta)

A Course 6 student in Simmons Hall has a research meeting in 32-G904, on the ninth floor of the Stata Center, and has a sprained ankle, so they open the app and tick "Step-free route only". They pick 8C (their floor in Simmons) as the start and type "32-G9" to find 32-G904 among the thousands of campus rooms. The app gives numbered steps: the Simmons C elevator down to the lobby, a few outdoor walks with distances and compass directions toward Building 36, in through 36 into Stata on the first floor, and up elevator E4 to G9. At Stata the E4 elevator is out of service, so they press "Flag blocked" on that step, add their name and "doors won't open", and the directions immediately switch to another Stata elevator. The report shows in the open problems list. Later someone presses "Mark as clear" on a different ride of the same elevator; the whole elevator comes back, and the report is resolved.
