## Findings
- **M1: An outline-less converter run can silently drop committed outlines.** The brief keeps outlines in `data/raw/drawings/outlines/<building>.json` on the local machine. The contract says the converter "adds them to the plan files, so they are committed with them", but never says what a converter run does when that local file is missing, for example on another machine or after the drawings are removed. As written, a regenerated plan file loses its outlines and the page quietly falls back to the schematic. The contract needs to state either that outlines persist where the converter can always find them, or that the converter keeps an existing outline when no new one is supplied, or that it deliberately drops them.
- **M2: The case of a room with no inventory area is unspecified.** The size check covers areas "within a factor of two" and says areas "under 3 m² are not checked". The schematic rule elsewhere admits rooms "with no area", and nothing says whether a traced outline for such a room is kept unchecked or dropped. The same question applies to a shared open area where some sharers have no area: is the combined area computed over the known areas only, or is the whole area rejected?
- **M3: The contract does not say how a drawing label becomes an inventory room.** Each room's space is "the open area around its label", but the contract does not say whether tracing uses the same misread-label recovery (the L/I/1, O/D/Q/0 confusion rule that requires exactly one unplaced match) that the plan positions use. It also does not say what happens to a label that matches no room, or a room whose label appears twice on a drawing. Without this, which room receives an outline, and whose area it is checked against, is ambiguous.
- **M4: The contract only says elevators, stairs and seated halls are "usually not traced".** The brief excludes tracing them. "Usually" leaves it open whether one of these that passes the size check is kept or rejected. The contract should state which: either such rooms are never traced, or they are kept like any other room when they happen to pass the check.

Not findings: the drawings staying local while outlines are committed matches the user's decision. Placing outlines into map coordinates through the drawing's fit is implied by "traced from each fitted drawing", which is acceptable.
